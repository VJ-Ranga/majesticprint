# Customer journeys

## Standard business order

Find product → choose valid specification/quantity → see total → upload artwork or choose approved design-help option → review line → checkout → verified payment → staff checks artwork → customer approves required proof → production → pickup/dispatch → completed.

Payment before staff proof is a **proposed policy**, not a confirmed rule. Explain it before checkout; confirm cancellation/refund handling when files are unsuitable. Production requires both cleared payment and all required artwork approval.

## Personal gift

Browse gifts/occasion → select product and supported personalisation → check image/text requirements → review illustrative preview → specify fulfilment → pay → staff check/proof where needed → produce → receive.

Do not let an attractive mockup imply exact print colour, cropping, or engraving accuracy. If an editor is deferred, provide a clear upload/text-input route and staff review.

## Custom business or installation project

Explain requirement → supply known specs/artwork and desired date → staff clarify feasibility → issue versioned quote → customer accepts → create payable order → collect agreed amount → complete proof/measurement approval → schedule production/installation → delivery/completion.

Quotes must disclose validity, delivery/installation exclusions, deposit terms if relevant, and whether a deadline is confirmed. Quote acceptance must not silently accept a later changed specification.

## Customer without artwork

Choose design help → provide brief, text, logos and reference material → understand design fee and revision policy → receive proof → approve correct version → proceed under the agreed payment/production policy.

## Repeat customer

Open previous order → review current availability/price → retain or replace artwork → verify delivery and deadline → submit as a new order. “Reorder” must not reuse an old approval when artwork changed.

## Recovery paths

Invalid option: explain allowed alternatives. Failed upload: preserve choices and allow retry. Unsupported file: explain accepted types/help. Expired quote: request revision. Failed/cancelled payment: preserve order and offer safe retry. Production hold: tell customer why and what action resolves it.

Related: [[Artwork and Proof Workflow]], [[Payments and Fulfilment]], [[QA and Acceptance]].

## Guide-led project routes

The quote path must handle UV substrate projects, packaging/dielines, CNC/laser drawings, signage surveys/lighting, vehicle photos/coverage and corporate-gift campaigns. Personal gifts retain a simple product route. The homepage and Solutions hub preselect the appropriate family while keeping “Help me choose” available.
