## Evidence rules

**Confirmed requirement** means the user stated it. **Observed** means it was present in a public source on the research date. **Proposed** means our recommendation. **Unverified** means it still needs business, admin, or runtime confirmation.

Competitor marketing claims are not independently verified performance. International companies are selected for relevant website patterns, not ranked as the world's best printers. Pricing from the old website is an observation, not an approved rate card.
