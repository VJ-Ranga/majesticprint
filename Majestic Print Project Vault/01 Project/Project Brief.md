# Project brief

## Confirmed requirements

- Redesign [majesticprint.lk](https://majesticprint.lk/).
- Plan and document the project in `/home/vjranga/Documents/majesticprint`.
- Create an Obsidian vault for findings, new information, decisions, and plans.
- Build an HTML site first; then move the approved experience to WordPress.
- The eventual project includes e-commerce and payment gateway integration.
- A substantial custom project with time and budget for thoughtful execution; no amount or deadline has been supplied.
- Visually distinctive, responsive, accessible, and practical on customer devices.
- Keep animation limited so customers can open and use it easily.
- Research Sri Lankan printing competitors and relevant international businesses.
- The current header carries the new logo. **BRAND GUIDELINES copy.pdf is now supplied and must govern identity, palette, typography and service coverage.**
- Latest request: revise the earlier information/research, create a proper sitemap/page tree and per-page structure, then build a homepage wireframe. These review deliverables are authorised; final storefront and production launch remain later stages.
- User clarified the audience: **a mix of business print orders and personal gifts**.

## Purpose

Help people find the right print product, understand its options and cost, supply usable artwork, and complete either a purchase or a well-specified quote request. Give Majestic's staff enough information to produce the correct job without repeated clarification.

## Proposed customer groups

Small businesses and repeat corporate buyers; individuals buying personalised gifts; wedding/event organisers; customers requiring signs, vehicle branding, or other installation work. These groups follow from advertised services, not order-data analysis.

## Proposed success measures

Measure product discovery, completed paid orders, usable quote requests, checkout abandonment, artwork resubmission frequency, quote response time, and reorders. Set numerical commercial targets after obtaining current analytics and staff data. Do not invent conversion-uplift promises.

Quality acceptance is defined in [[QA and Acceptance]]. Operational correctness matters as much as appearance: the correct artwork version must reach staff with the correct options and payment state.

## Scope boundary for this phase

Research, guide reconciliation, sitemap/page structures and a homepage wireframe are review deliverables. Identity and brand colour tokens are established by the guide. Detailed rates, operational policies, font files/licences, plugin selection, implementation schedules and final storefront designs remain pending. No production edits, customer-data access, payments or launch have occurred.
