# Recommended experience

## Three approaches

| Approach | Strength | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Product-led shop with guided custom quotes — recommended** | Serves business print and gifts; clear buying routes; reusable page system | Requires a properly structured catalogue and rate card |
| B. Premium portfolio with enquiries first | Strong showcase for bespoke and installation work | More staff effort; weak self-service for standard products |
| C. Full online design platform | Templates and interactive personalisation may help some products | Higher licensing, integration, performance and production complexity |

Choose A as the proposed base. Use quality photography and case studies from B. Evaluate selected editor features from C only after core ordering is reliable.

## Proposed visual direction

A professional, modern, reliable and creative all-in-one print, packaging, branding and promotional company, following the supplied guide. Use corporate deep blue `#13477A`, with restrained bright blue `#007CAC`, magenta `#D01C60`, orange `#CF6428` and gold `#EAA123`. Use light supporting surfaces and real materials to keep the extensive offer easy to browse. See [[Brand Guide Findings]] for typography roles and colour pairing limits.

Use one primary action colour, a clearly distinct quote/help treatment, and consistent cards. Show paper edges, print detail, mugs, apparel, signs and finished projects. Use actual live text for headlines and product benefits. Product imagery should convey craft without delaying access to the catalogue.

Premium value should come from photography, layout, comparison tools, helpful copy, and predictable interactions. Keep motion limited to short feedback transitions. No content should depend on animation completing.

## Proposed homepage promise

Brand-led headline: **Print. Pack. Promote.**

Supporting brand statement: **One partner. Every printing solution.** The earlier alternative slogan is superseded. Product shopping and personal gifts remain visible within the full company positioning.

Support it with a concise introduction to business print and personalised products. The exact wording and any claims about production quality, speed, delivery or design support require client review.

Primary CTA: **Shop products**. Secondary CTA: **Plan a custom project**. Print / Pack / Promote routes, selected products and the 12-family Solutions hub preserve the full capability offer. Gifts are visible within Promote and the product grid.
