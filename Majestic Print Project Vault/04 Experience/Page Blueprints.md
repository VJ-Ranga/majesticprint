# Page blueprints

## Homepage

1. H01: shared header with logo, Shop/Solutions navigation, quote and planned search/account/cart utilities.
2. H02: static **Print. Pack. Promote.** hero, real product photography, shop and custom-project routes.
3. H03: Print / Pack / Promote purpose routes for the mixed audience.
4. H04: selected catalogue products; only publish confirmed quantities, specifications and prices.
5. H05: specialist capabilities and a link to all 12 solution families.
6. H06: standard-product and custom-project ordering paths; artwork help.
7. H07: two or three customer-approved projects with production details.
8. H08: guide-led company statement, facility/team image and verified company evidence.
9. H09: custom-project invitation and service-aware quote route.
10. H10: contact, hours, help, delivery, policies and verified social accounts.

Seasonal content should be client-managed and date-aware. Do not leave Christmas promotions permanently prominent without a current campaign.

## Category / search

Product grid; meaningful filters; concise product comparisons; sort; visible result count; search correction/no-result help; mobile filter panel. Cards must clearly separate configurable purchases from quote-based work. Do not use unqualified “from” prices.

## Standard product

Gallery, clear product name/benefit, exact dimensions, options grouped in a sensible order, quantity tiers, order total and optional unit price, artwork route, production/delivery explanation, primary purchase action, bulk/custom alternative. Follow with materials comparison, specifications, artwork download, relevant FAQs and verified reviews.

At small widths, gallery and configuration stack vertically. A sticky action is optional and must never hide errors, focus, or required options.

## Personalised gift product

Product/colour/size choices, text or image customisation when supported, preview limitation, gifting options if operationally available, required artwork and deadline information. Explain whether a preview is illustrative or staff-approved.

## Custom service / quote

Real work images; scope of the service; examples; relevant dimensions, material, quantity, artwork, destination/installation details and desired date. Unknown dimensions/materials must have an “I'm not sure” route. State the expected next step; only show a response-time promise the team can maintain.

## Cart and checkout

Cart line identifies specification, artwork state/version and quantity. Edits preserve the user's choices. Show delivery, fees and taxes transparently. Guest checkout is the proposed default. Gateway failure/cancellation returns to a recoverable order summary without implying payment succeeded.

## Account / status

Separate payment status, artwork/proof status, production and dispatch. Provide current next action and support reference. A reorder starts from prior specifications but rechecks availability, price and artwork suitability.
