## Shop versus solutions

**Shop** answers “What do I want to order?” using products/occasions. **Solutions** answers “Can Majestic do this kind of project?” using all 12 guide families. A business card is one customer product even when several print processes can produce it. Production method is a specification or staff recommendation, not necessarily a duplicate SKU/page.

Legacy advertising materials are not listed as a standalone guide family. Retain them in the migration register and confirm whether they remain a stock collection. Do not delete them based only on omission from the new guide.
