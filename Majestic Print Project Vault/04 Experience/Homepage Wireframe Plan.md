# Homepage wireframe plan

Review artifact: `planning/homepage-wireframe.html` in the workspace root. This is an annotated, responsive layout study with guide colours and representative copy. It is not the finished HTML storefront or a working commerce site.

## Section tree

```text
Home /
├── H01 Shared header
│   ├── Approved logo location / brand tagline
│   ├── Shop · Solutions · Our work · Help · About · Contact
│   └── Search / account / cart locations + Request a quote
├── H02 Hero
│   ├── Print. Pack. Promote.
│   ├── All-in-one business and personal print introduction
│   ├── Shop products + Plan a custom project
│   └── Real photography slot: print kit + packaging + gift/sign example
├── H03 Print / Pack / Promote entry points
│   └── Three clear routes with examples and next actions
├── H04 Browse popular products
│   └── Six representative product slots; validated rates later
├── H05 Custom capability spotlight
│   ├── UV / large format / signage
│   ├── CNC / laser / vehicle branding
│   └── All 12 solutions link
├── H06 How ordering works
│   ├── Standard order: choose → artwork → review/pay → produce
│   └── Complex project: brief → quote → approval → produce/install
├── H07 Selected work
│   └── Print / packaging / signage project evidence slots
├── H08 One partner. Every printing solution.
│   └── Company/team/process evidence; no invented award figures
├── H09 Project invitation
│   └── Request a quote / Contact; business and personal examples
└── H10 Shared footer
    └── Shop, solutions, help, company, policies and verified contacts
```

## Content and layout decisions

H02 leads with the brand tagline instead of the earlier proposed replacement slogan. H03 gives packaging/promotional work equal conceptual visibility to print. H04 is a selected product set, not an asserted bestseller ranking. H05 stops specialised capability being buried under a generic gift shop. H07/H08 reserve real evidence, without fabricated customer logos, awards, reviews or counts.

The wireframe uses neutral placeholders for unavailable photos and rates. Product/section links navigate within the review files or page-plan tree. Search/cart/account are labelled planned functions, not deceptive working controls. No form sends data.

## Responsive rules

Desktop: two-column hero, three purpose routes, three-column selected-product grid, restrained section spacing. Mobile: expandable navigation, single-column hero/routes/content, two-column products only while cards remain legible. The order and essential CTAs stay consistent. No carousel, scroll animation, autoplay video or forced popup.

## Brand execution

Use exact guide colour tokens. Keep the logo reference unchanged. Declare Nexa/Swiss font roles but identify the fallback until licensed standalone fonts are supplied. Normal text uses pairings validated in [[Brand Guide Findings]]. The wireframe's layout is a proposal, not a reproduction of the tall PDF's page layout.

## Review questions

Assess homepage priorities, routes and section order; product relevance; balance of business, personal and specialist work; expected CTA destination. Final photos, font metrics, rate labels and trust copy are a later design/content pass.
