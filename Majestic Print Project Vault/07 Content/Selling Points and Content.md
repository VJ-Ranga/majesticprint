# Selling points and content

Lead with the supplied guide’s **Print. Pack. Promote.** and **One partner. Every printing solution.** Position Majestic as an all-in-one printing, packaging, branding and promotional provider with the guide’s professional, modern and reliable personality. Product outcomes and verified work should substantiate quality. [[Service Coverage Matrix]] defines the 12 families that copy must cover.

## Claim-to-evidence register

| Candidate message | Evidence needed before publication |
|---|---|
| Experienced printing team | Confirmed founding date and team/company history |
| Award-winning quality | Award certificates, years/categories and permitted presentation |
| Print for business and personal occasions | Approved active product catalogue |
| Help with design and artwork | Staff capability, fee/revision policy and check scope |
| Reliable delivery/pickup | Coverage, carrier/rates, lead time and service conditions |
| Small runs and bulk quantities | Product-specific minimums and rate tiers |
| Local production and service | Verified facility/location and actual fulfilment model |
| Sustainable choices | Supplier/certification evidence and specific applicable products |

None of these should be promoted as a guarantee solely because competitors use similar language. Do not claim “lowest price,” exact colour matching, 24-hour service, free design, or islandwide/next-day delivery without approval and operational evidence.

## Content to commission

- Real product photographs: wide view, scale, reverse/inside, and finish detail.
- Short product descriptions: intended use, material, exact dimensions, options, artwork and turnaround.
- Three to five initial project stories, subject to client/customer permission.
- Staff/company story and production/process photos.
- Practical guides: preparing artwork, choosing paper/finish, understanding production and delivery.
- Product-specific FAQs and approved policy copy.

Proposed collection ideas: business essentials, event/wedding stationery, gifts by occasion, and labels for small brands. Approve their products and pricing before using them as purchasable bundles.

## Copy principles

Use everyday buyer language. Explain terms such as GSM, lamination and bleed where selection requires them. Prefer an accurate pack price to an unexplained per-piece teaser. Keep policies easy to find before payment. Do not copy third-party product text, images, testimonials or brand styling.

## Ownership

Client approves brand, catalogue, claim evidence and policy. Production staff approve technical specifications. Project team structures and edits copy. Maintain a content status for each launch page: draft, technically reviewed, client-approved, implemented, verified.

## Expanded content commission

Collect examples of packaging, UV surfaces, 3D/illuminated signs, CNC/laser work, branded vehicles and corporate gift sets as well as paper print and personal gifts. For each family, write outputs, materials/limits, preparation requirements and quote inputs. Preserve the guide tagline; the earlier alternative homepage slogan is superseded.
