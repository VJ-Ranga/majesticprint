## Proposed Sri Lankan selling priorities

1. **Clarity of price:** LKR totals, explicit pack/unit quantities, and delivery charges before payment.
2. **Help with artwork:** explain file requirements, offer design help, and show when approval is needed.
3. **Confidence in arrival:** distinguish artwork readiness, print lead time, and courier time.
4. **Real quality:** close-up products, actual projects, verified awards and reviews.
5. **Flexible support:** a useful website with a visible WhatsApp/call fallback.
6. **Repeat-order convenience:** make previous specifications easy to revisit, with prices and stock rechecked.

Validate these with staff and customers. We have not established demand levels, competitor market shares, regional language preference, or willingness to pay.

## Worth considering later

Sample packs, business starter bundles, gift occasion collections, saved jobs and repeat orders remain optional enhancements. Packaging is **confirmed as a guide service family** and needs a launch solution/quote page; sellable packaging SKUs and rates remain unconfirmed. A template editor is optional.

## Popups and promotions

No cross-site popup audit was performed. For Majestic, use inline help and dismissible operational notices. Avoid automatic entry promotions, competing floating widgets, autoplay media and artificial countdowns. A customer-triggered preview or finish explanation is useful; interruption is not a design goal.
