# Research method and limits

## What was done

- Scanned the exact requested workspace, including hidden folders; no initial project/brand files were available.
- Retrieved public Majestic homepage, about, contact, FAQ, all-products, shop, two product pages and three service pages, plus an empty-cart checkout response.
- Parsed public page/product/service sitemaps and extracted URL counts/inventory.
- Observed homepage browser rendering at desktop/mobile overrides; saved two viewport screenshots. Overrides were reset afterwards.
- Identified the public header SVG, preserving an unmodified reference.
- Read official local/international website pages or official search excerpts; recorded failed/inconsistent sources.
- Checked official WooCommerce, PayHere, W3C and web.dev documentation for planning constraints.

## Verification boundaries

The current site was not changed. No contact/quote/review form was submitted, account created, artwork uploaded, cart item added or payment attempted. No authenticated WordPress/plugin/configuration or database inspection occurred. No customer/private files were accessed.

Current-site content observations are based mainly on returned HTML. Browser checks cover only sample homepage rendering, not every page or the entire ordering journey. Screenshots capture animation/loading at one moment; they do not establish a benchmark or accessibility compliance. Homepage source and rendered visibility differed in the sampled session; the cause was not diagnosed.

Competitor inspection primarily covers website text and service positioning. No systematic visual ranking, popup timing study, mobile-performance comparison or completed competitor checkout was conducted. Company claims are attributed, not endorsed.

Majestic was fetched successfully through direct HTTP after web-tool fetch failed. InoPrintech remained unavailable after a direct retry; its data is a search lead. Print Right's search and fetched content conflicted; the claimed customiser is unverified. Printcare Agile's homepage direct fetch timed out; official search excerpts/services supplied limited evidence.

## Retained evidence

`current-url-inventory.json`: sitemap URLs and proposed handling. `public-page-observations.json`: sample observations and methods. `Screenshots/`: two homepage viewport captures. `Brand Reference/`: public header SVG. These are dated snapshots, not permanent truth.

No production storefront has been built or deployed. A responsive homepage wireframe and sitemap viewer now exist as local design-review artifacts; their browser checks are recorded separately. [[QA and Acceptance]] still defines future commerce/runtime acceptance.
