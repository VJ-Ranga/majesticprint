# Open questions

These questions are tracked for upcoming decisions; the research deliverable does not depend on answering all of them now.

## First decisions

1. Guide received/reviewed: supply approved master logo variants and standalone licensed font files, confirming the production equivalent of Nexa Text-Trial.
2. Confirm the product-led shop plus custom-quote direction in [[Recommended Experience]].
3. Identify the first 8–12 launch products and which need online prices versus quotes.
4. Provide the current rate card with quantities, materials, sizes, tax treatment and exclusions.
5. Confirm the current gateway, merchant arrangements and whether it should be retained.

## Ordering and staff

What products require proofs? Is payment taken before proof review or after approval? What happens if artwork is unsuitable? Who owns checks/design/revisions, and what is charged? Are quotes paid in full or by deposit? Can customers submit without artwork? How are refunds, cancellations and incorrect customer content handled?

Confirm exact production stages, staff roles, enquiry response targets, notifications and artwork retention. Do not assume a full browser designer is required; determine which personalisation genuinely needs one.

## Catalogue and fulfilment

The guide confirms the 12 service families, including CNC, laser, UV and packaging. Which specific configurations are sellable online, and do legacy materials remain in the shop? Is “Marketplace” just the shop or a separate business? Which minimum quantities and custom finishes apply? What can be couriered, collected, or installed? What are the location, hours, delivery coverage and rates? Can urgent work be promised, and under what conditions?

## Brand, language and content

Are English, Sinhala and Tamil needed at launch? Which approved photos, projects, reviews and award records are available? What is the founding date/legal name? Which social accounts and contacts are current? Who approves commercial and policy wording?

## Implementation and release

Confirm budget range, target date, hosting/staging access, existing editor preference, customer/order data preservation, email/analytics integrations, maintenance owner and licence budget. Do staff need corporate reorders/workspaces at launch or later?
