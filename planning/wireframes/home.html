H06 / Ordering guidance

A clear path from idea to print.

Two paths reduce uncertainty. Payment and proof policy must be confirmed; production requires cleared payment and any required artwork approval.

Ordering a standard product

  1. Choose your product and specification.
  2. Provide artwork or request design help.
  3. Review the order and payment details.
  4. Complete required approval, then production.

Planning something custom

  1. Tell us the intended result.
  2. Review the specification and quote.
  3. Agree the artwork and project requirements.
  4. Proceed to production and fulfilment.
