Print. Pack. Promote.

Sitemap & page structures

A proposed website tree for business printing and personal gifts, aligned with the supplied brand guide. Open each page to review its purpose, primary action and ordered sections.

The shop uses customer needs; the Solutions hub covers all 12 brand-guide service families. Product templates and private customer routes are shown as patterns, not approved live URLs. Prices, gateway, fulfilment rules and publishable product specifications still need confirmation.

Brand colours are applied here. Licensed web fonts have not been supplied; the review uses Arial fallbacks. This is a review artifact, with no working checkout or customer submissions.
